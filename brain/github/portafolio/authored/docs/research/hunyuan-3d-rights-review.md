# Hunyuan 3D rights review

Reviewed: 2026-07-19  
Status: Rejected for production use under the reviewed terms  
Scope: worldwide public portfolio asset generation and display

## Reviewed source

- Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.1 Community License Agreement: `https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/blob/main/LICENSE`
- Model release date stated by the license: 2025-06-13.
- Hosted Studio creation page checked: `https://3d.hunyuan.tencent.com/studio/creation/concept`.

## Blocking terms

The community license defines its territory as worldwide excluding the European Union, United Kingdom, and South Korea. It limits the rights grant to that territory and says use, reproduction, modification, distribution, or display of the model works, outputs, or results outside the territory is unlicensed and unauthorized.

That restriction conflicts with a public portfolio intended to be accessible worldwide. The hosted Studio may use separate terms, but those terms were not publicly retrievable during this review, so the Studio is not an approved workaround.

## Project decision

- Do not use Hunyuan3D 2.1 or unverified Hunyuan Studio outputs in the production portfolio.
- Do not upload Carlos's reference images to the hosted service until its applicable terms and privacy behavior are archived and approved.
- Keep the runtime and asset manifest provider-neutral.
- Use procedural Three.js, Carlos-owned/manual assets, commissioned work, or clearly permissive assets with provenance.
- Reopen only through a new decision record if the applicable license or written permission changes.

This document records a project risk decision and is not legal advice.
