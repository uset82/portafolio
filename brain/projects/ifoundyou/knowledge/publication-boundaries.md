# Publication boundaries

- Say **prototype** or **experimental project**, never shipped safety product.
- Say it explores Web Meshtastic and native peer-to-peer paths, never that it reliably works
  without internet.
- Do not claim emergency-service affiliation, verified alerts, identity verification,
  moderation coverage, or life-critical suitability.
- Do not claim sole authorship, production adoption, users, uptime, field tests, or measured
  privacy/security outcomes.
- Do not reuse repository screenshots, map captures, launcher assets, contacts, coordinates, or
  schema images without a separate rights and privacy approval.
