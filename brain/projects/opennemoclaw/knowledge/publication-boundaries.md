# Publication boundaries

- Say **prototype**, never released, production-safe, hardened, or completely isolated.
- Do not advertise the README's broken one-line installer URLs or describe a public package or
  live demo.
- Do not call the distributed repository MIT-licensed until a license file is present and
  detected.
- Do not claim provider-agnostic support beyond the verified OpenRouter and generic HTTP
  connector code.
- Do not claim sole authorship, adoption, performance, reliability, security results, or a
  production case study.
- Do not reuse the repository hero or other project media until provenance and permission are
  documented.

Source: `docs/content/opennemoclaw-source-pack.md`.
